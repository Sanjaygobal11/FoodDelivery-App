🍔 About Foodie Express

Foodie Express is an online food delivery application developed using Django and Python.

Users can browse foods, add items to cart, place orders and view order history.

✨ Features

  • 🍕 Food Categories
  • 🛒 Cart System
  • 📦 Order History
  • 💳 Checkout & Payment
  • 📱 Responsive Design
